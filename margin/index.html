Install on iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
Books, highlights and reading positions are kept on this device and work offline. Margin opens DRM-free EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, PDF, Word (.docx), comics (CBZ, CBR, CB7) and plain-text books, and audiobooks (M4B, MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC; select all of a book’s files together, or add its .zip). Drag a file onto the window, use Open with from your file manager, or share a book to Margin from another app. Press ? while reading for keyboard shortcuts.
